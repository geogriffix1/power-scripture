### The Lord met Moses and was about to Kill him

Exodus 4:24-26 is a very odd passage of scripture. God has spoken to Moses through the burning bush and commissioned Moses to return to Egypt from his Midianite father-in-law's land. Moses takes his family with him.

On the way, God approaches Moses and is about to kill him, but Zipporah, the wife of Moses, takes a piece of flint as a blade and circumcises their son, Gershom. She removes the foreskin from Gershom and touches it to Moses's foot. That satisfies God.

God has a covenant of circumcision with Abraham and all his descendants. Israel's children are supposed to be circumcised when they are eight days old. Zipporah is a Midianite. Midianites are also children of Abraham so they also have the covenant of circumcision. Zipporah knew what to do when Moses apparently did not.

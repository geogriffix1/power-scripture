Genesis 20 is an odd scripture, partly because of where it is placed in the text of scripture and partly because of its similarity with Genesis 12:10-20 and also with Genesis chapter 26. In Genesis 18:10, Abraham is told that a year from now his wife, Sarah, will bear Isaac. Sarah bears Isaac in Genesis 21:2. So Genesis 20 is placed in the Bible in the span of one year between the coming of the three angels who destroy Sodom and Gomorrah and the birth of Isaac.

Chapter 20 tells us that Abraham tells King Abimelek that Sarah is his sister. Sarah becomes a part of Abimelek's harem but God did not allow him to have sex with her. God spoke to Abimelek in a dream telling him that Sarah is a married woman and there would be a curse on him. During the time Abimelek's wives and concubines were not able to to conceive children. The curse was lifted when Sarah was returned to Abraham.

One thing particularly odd about this passage is that Sarah would have been 79 or 80 years old and pregnant based on where the story appears in the Bible. Abraham reveals in that chapter that Sarah actually is a half-sister to him, so calling her a sister is true.

In Genesis chapter 12 a similar thing had already happened in Egypt with Pharoah. In Genesis chapter 20 something similar happens between a king also named Abimelek, but with Isaac, the son of Abraham, and his wife Rebeka.

In my humble opinion, Genesis Chapter 20 is a probably a version of the narrative of Genesis 26 with Isaac and Rebeka.

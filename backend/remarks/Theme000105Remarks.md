## Cain & Abel
### Jealousy, Murder, Curse, Blessing, and Embellishment

Abel and Cain were children of Adam and Eve. Abel's sacrifice was accepted by God but Cain's was not. Out of jealousy, Cain murdered Abel and tried to hide the sin from God.

God placed a curse on Cain that his crops would never bear any fruit no matter what he did, and he could never live in one place but would have to wander from place to place. Cain appealed to God that people would kill him. God put a mark on Cain. It was a mark of protection. Anyone who kills Cain will experience seven times the brutality from God as they applied to Cain.

We don't know what the mark of Cain was. Some have postulated that God darkened Cain's skin, and that Cain's descendants carried the same mark. They consider the mark to be a curse and a justification for mistreating people, when in fact the mark is a blessing. It is protection that God placed over Cain. People have free will - so someone might mistreat Cain but God's retribution would be upon them.

A descendant of Cain named Lamech murdered a young man. He embellished the blessing of Cain, telling his wives that since Cain was protected by God with a seven-fold protection that he, Lamech, should therefore be protected by God seventy-seven times. - *George Griffin*

By the way, the descendants of Cain died in a flood.

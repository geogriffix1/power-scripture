There is an interesting little quirk in the biblical account of the selling of Joseph to the caravan traveling to Egypt in Genesis 37. The text flips from calling the merchants Ishmaelites and calling them Midianites.

Israelites, Ishmaelites, and Midianites are all tribes of Abraham. Israel is Jacob the grandson of Abraham. Ishmael is the son of Abraham and Hagar. Midian is the son of Abraham and Keturah.

Some would say that the caravan was a mix of Ishmaelites and Midianites. I think more likely, this is an example of two different oral traditions being merged together.
